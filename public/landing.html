How it works

  1. 1

    Upload the slice

    Paste HTML or drop a zip/folder of your front-end build. Write the task you want tested.

  2. 2

    Share the link

    Send it to teammates, users, or a community. Testers open it in a browser — anonymous, consent-gated, no account, no install.

  3. 3

    Watch what happened

    Replays, heatmaps, and success signals land on your results page as responses come in.

Everything you need to watch someone actually use it

⇪

You are the runtime

Pull straight from a GitHub repo, paste self-contained HTML, or upload a zip or folder of your build. Integral hosts it and hands you a live test link.

▶

Session replay & heatmaps

Every session is recorded with rrweb. Scrub through replays of real attempts, and see click heatmaps of where people actually went.

✓

Measured task success

Give testers a task and define what success looks like. Get completion rates, timings, and full JSON/CSV export.

◑

Sentiment scored

Every comment and post-test note gets a polarity score from a word lexicon.

The whole loop, start to finish
